SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a company having its registered office in Hyderabad, India ("Service Provider"), and XYZ Solutions Pvt. Ltd., a company having its registered office in Bengaluru, India ("Client"), collectively referred to as the "Parties."

1. EFFECTIVE DATE

This Agreement shall be effective as of 15 August 2026 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue for a period of 1 year, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the terms below.

2. SERVICES

The Service Provider shall provide software development and maintenance services to the Client, including but not limited to design, development, testing, and deployment of software applications, as described in the applicable statement of work.

3. PAYMENT

The Client shall pay ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd. a service fee of ₹250000 per month, payable within 30 days of receipt of a valid invoice. A late payment penalty of 2% per month shall apply to any amount not paid within the due date.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party agrees to keep confidential all confidential information disclosed by the other party during the term of this Agreement, and shall not disclose such confidential information to any third party without prior written consent.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All intellectual property, including software, source code, and documentation created by the Service Provider under this Agreement shall remain the exclusive property of the Client upon full payment, except for pre-existing proprietary tools of the Service Provider.

6. LIABILITY

In no event shall either party's liability under this Agreement exceed the total fees paid in the preceding six (6) months. Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising out of this Agreement.

7. INDEMNIFICATION

The Service Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless the Client from any third-party claims arising out of gross negligence or willful misconduct by the Service Provider in the performance of the services.

8. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing 30-day written notice to the other party. Either party may also terminate this Agreement immediately for cause in the event of a material breach that remains uncured for 15 days after written notice.

9. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL

This Agreement shall automatically renew for additional periods of one year unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to the end of the then-current term.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall first be referred to mediation, and if not resolved within 30 days, shall be finally resolved through binding arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act.

11. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

12. INSURANCE

The Service Provider shall maintain professional indemnity insurance coverage of not less than ₹5000000 throughout the term of this Agreement.

13. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance under this Agreement to the extent such failure or delay is caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, natural disasters, war, or governmental action.

14. GENERAL OBLIGATIONS

Each party shall be responsible for complying with all applicable laws and regulations in the performance of its obligations under this Agreement, and shall ensure that its personnel are adequately qualified to perform the services described herein.

15. NOTICE

Any notice required under this Agreement shall be given in writing and delivered by email or courier to the address of the receiving party specified in this Agreement. Notice shall be deemed effective upon receipt.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date written above.
